# PRD 002 — GPS Reliability and Map Debugging

## Goal
Make location capture trustworthy enough for field testing and make every saved coordinate easy to verify visually.

## Scope
- Request high-accuracy GPS with `maximumAge: 0`.
- Watch multiple location readings for up to 20 seconds.
- Keep the most accurate fix instead of accepting the first reading.
- Stop early when accuracy reaches 15 meters or better.
- Save latitude, longitude, accuracy, altitude when available, and location timestamp.
- Show coordinates in a large, high-contrast block.
- Show GPS quality as Excellent, Good, or Poor.
- Make the entire coordinate block open Google Maps at the exact point.
- Apply the same large tappable coordinate block to every saved recording.
- Keep copy coordinates and delete actions.

## Quality thresholds
- Excellent: 15 meters or better.
- Good: 16–50 meters.
- Poor: worse than 50 meters.

## Acceptance criteria
- A new recording stores the best GPS fix received during the acquisition window.
- Coordinate text is readable outdoors on a phone.
- Tapping coordinates opens Google Maps centered on the saved latitude and longitude.
- Accuracy is visible before and after saving.
- Existing saved recordings remain readable.
