# PRD 002 — GPS Reliability and Map Verification

## Status
Delivered, v0.4.1.

## Goal
Make location capture trustworthy enough for field testing and make every saved coordinate easy to verify visually on a map.

## Scope

- Request high-accuracy GPS with `enableHighAccuracy: true`, `maximumAge: 0`, and `timeout: 20000`.
- Watch multiple location readings for up to 20 seconds.
- Keep the most accurate fix instead of accepting the first reading.
- Stop early when accuracy reaches 15 meters or better.
- Clear the watch when the window closes so the device does not keep sampling.
- Save latitude, longitude, accuracy, altitude (when available), and the location timestamp with each recording.
- Show a live acquisition status while the window is open, so the operator can see GPS working.
- Show coordinates in a large, high-contrast block.
- Show GPS quality as Excellent, Good, or Poor.
- Make the entire coordinate block open Google Maps at the exact point.
- Apply the same large tappable coordinate block to every saved recording.
- Keep copy-coordinates and delete actions.

## Maps URL contract

Every coordinate card links to:

```
https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=LAT,LNG
```

The latitude and longitude are appended at the end with a comma between them, no spaces.

## Transient-error rule

GPS errors fall into two categories:

- **Abort the acquisition:** `PERMISSION_DENIED` (code `1`). The user has refused location, so retrying will not help.
- **Keep going:** `POSITION_UNAVAILABLE` (code `2`) and `TIMEOUT` (code `3`). The next reading may still be a good one, so the acquisition window continues until the 20-second ceiling.

Only `PERMISSION_DENIED` aborts. Everything else is treated as transient.

## Quality thresholds

- **Excellent:** 15 meters or better.
- **Good:** 16 to 50 meters.
- **Poor:** worse than 50 meters.

## Acceptance criteria

- A new recording stores the best GPS fix received during the acquisition window, not the first one.
- The acquisition window closes at the 20-second ceiling or earlier if a fix of 15 meters or better arrives.
- A transient `POSITION_UNAVAILABLE` or `TIMEOUT` does not abort the window.
- A `PERMISSION_DENIED` does abort the window.
- Coordinates, accuracy, altitude (when available), acquisition time, and quality label are visible on every recording card.
- Coordinate text is readable outdoors on a phone.
- Tapping a coordinate card opens Google Maps centered on the saved latitude and longitude using the documented URL form.
- Accuracy is visible both during acquisition and after the recording has been saved.
- Existing saved recordings remain readable after the change.
